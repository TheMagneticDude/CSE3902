Sprint 2 Reflection

Near the end of the sprint, write up a brief report on how your team performed this sprint, 
using the burndown chart from the board as the basis for discussion. Feel free to also discuss 
your processes as a whole. Were any changes your made this sprint constructive or detrimental? 
Do you have any plans for doing things differently next sprint?

Overall, I think our team performed well this sprint and managed to implement everything we
planned on and more. Since we decided to create a game different from the mario and zelda options,
we had more planning that needed to be done as far as the scope of our project. We kind of just
decided on general ideas for our project and started implementing the different features based on 
tasks we put in the issues section of github, but I think going into the next sprint we need to think
about the actual scope of our project a bit more and what our end goal will look like. I think we did 
a good job as a team of splitting the tasks up and each doing our share of the work, and also helping
each other with parts of the code as needed. For the next sprint we also need to work on refactoring 
our code somewhat and have better organization, maybe using the command design pattern just to make
sure that we have quality code practices before our project gets too complex. Another thing we plan
to do differently next sprint is having better management of branches and pull requests, and adding
more constructive feedback when approving requests.
