Sprint 3 Reflection

I believe our team performed very well this sprint, we have proper collision and more importantly a dynamic 
system for hitboxes, the boss rooms are more fleshed out and the hotbar has a GUI.  Since the last sprint we 
have better defined the scope of our project and have a good roadmap for features needed to be implemented 
moving forward. Moving forwards on future sprints it would be ideal to refactor parts of our code, mainly 
IPlayer and IBoss ideally should have an interface that handles both of them so code can be iterated other 
both types of objects (maybe IEntity). We also plan to make the rooms much more detailed and fix the backgrounds,
we just have very basic rooms right now while we test everything.
